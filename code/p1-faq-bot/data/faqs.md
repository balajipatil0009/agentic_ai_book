## Shipping and Delivery

We ship to all 27 EU countries and the UK. Standard delivery takes 3-5 business
days and costs EUR 4.90. Orders above EUR 49 qualify for free standard
shipping. Express delivery (next business day) costs EUR 12.90 and is available
for orders placed before 14:00 CET. Tracking links are emailed within one hour
of dispatch.

## Returns and Refunds

You can return any unopened product within 30 days of delivery for a full
refund. Opened electronics can be returned within 14 days if there is a
manufacturing defect. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within
5 business days of us receiving the returned item. Return shipping is free for
defective items and costs EUR 4.90 otherwise.

## Warranty

All Nimbus Gadgets products carry a 24-month manufacturer warranty covering
defects in materials and workmanship. The warranty does not cover accidental
damage, water damage, or batteries degraded below 70% capacity after 12 months.
Warranty claims require the original order number and a description of the
fault. Approved claims are repaired or replaced within 10 business days.

## NimbusPulse Pro Watch (SKU NG-4200)

The NimbusPulse Pro (SKU NG-4200) costs EUR 249.00 and comes in midnight black,
silver, and ocean blue. Battery life is up to 14 days with typical use, 9 days
with always-on display. Water resistant to 5 ATM. Sensors: optical heart rate,
SpO2, GPS, accelerometer. Works with iOS 16+ and Android 11+. The watch is
ships with a magnetic charging cable; the fast-charge dock (SKU NG-4201, EUR
39.00) is sold separately.

## NimbusSound Buds (SKU NG-1150)

The NimbusSound Buds (SKU NG-1150) cost EUR 129.00. Active noise cancellation
reduces ambient noise by up to 32 dB. Total battery life is 30 hours with the
charging case (8 hours per charge). They support Bluetooth 5.3 multipoint, USB-C
charging, and wireless charging. The Buds are rated IPX4 (splash resistant), not
suitable for swimming.

## NimbusCharge Duo (SKU NG-780)

The NimbusCharge Duo (SKU NG-780) is a 15W dual wireless charging pad priced at
EUR 79.00. It charges two devices simultaneously: a phone at up to 15W and
earbuds at up to 5W. It requires a 30W USB-C power adapter, which is NOT
included in the box. Compatible with Qi and Qi2 devices.

## Account and Orders

You can track any order at nimbusgadgets.example/orders using your order number
(e.g. NG-2026-000123) and the email address used at checkout. To change a
shipping address, contact support before the order shows as "dispatched" —
after dispatch the address cannot be changed but the carrier may allow
redirects. Order invoices are downloadable from your account for 24 months.
